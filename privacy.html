Privacy notice

Taqyon Personal Automation · Effective 28 October 2026

Scope and operator

Fiqri Farish operates these tools for his own authorised Google accounts and projects. This notice covers the private automation tools described on the homepage and their Google API connections. They are not a public sign-up service. Contact: t4qy0n@gmail.com.

Information accessed

  • Account and connection information: the authorised account's email address, granted permissions, access and refresh credentials, and Drive storage quota, as needed to establish and verify access.
  • Drive-only backup connection: encrypted backup objects and their names, sizes, identifiers, integrity information and timestamps. The local backup process accesses the owner's selected source files to encrypt them before upload. It is not authorised to read all existing Drive files.
  • Drive-only project connection: selected owner-approved project documents, file contents and publication metadata. Access is limited by the granted app-file permission and any files explicitly made available to the app.
  • Separately authorised Workspace utilities: where requested by the owner and permitted by that client's consent, email content and metadata, calendar events, contact details, documents or spreadsheets needed for the requested task. The Drive-only clients do not inherit those permissions.

Purposes and use

Data is used to carry out owner-requested productivity tasks, maintain selected document publications, verify results, store and recover encrypted backups, and diagnose connection or integrity failures. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used by these tools to train general-purpose AI models.

When the owner requests AI-assisted work, the information necessary for that task may be processed by owner-configured AI service providers. This is distinct from backup transport: the backup transport does not send decrypted backup contents to an AI provider. Provider handling is subject to the applicable service terms and account settings; this notice does not promise that third-party services are offline or have no operational retention.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

Working documents, connection credentials and relevant operational records are stored in the owner's controlled systems. Credentials are kept outside this public website and restricted locally. When the backup system becomes operational, repository contents will be encrypted before upload to Google Drive; file sizes, object identifiers and transfer metadata can remain visible to the storage provider. Google account authentication is handled by Google; this website does not collect account passwords.

No security control guarantees against every loss, compromise or account revocation. The backup system is being prepared and must pass verification and isolated recovery tests before it is described as operational.

Sharing and service providers

Data is not published merely by connecting an account. The tools transfer data to Google to perform authorised service operations, and to owner-configured infrastructure or AI providers only as necessary for the requested work. The owner may explicitly direct a document to be shared with a recipient. Access may also be necessary for security, applicable legal obligations, or owner-directed support. This informational site does not expose private project material or backups.

Retention and removal

Connection credentials are retained while a connection is in use. Working documents and operational records follow the owner's project and security requirements; disconnecting Google does not automatically delete existing local documents or backup copies.

The approved intended backup policy retains seven daily recovery points locally and the overlapping selection of seven daily, four weekly and three monthly recovery points in Drive. Expired recovery points are to be removed automatically after required safety checks; integrity or backup failures can pause removal and trigger an alert. Any extra pre-upgrade protection must be bounded and separately approved. These are implementation requirements, not a claim that retention automation is already enabled.

The owner can revoke an app's access using Google Account third-party connections and request local credential, document or backup removal through the support contact. Revocation prevents future authorised access but does not itself erase previously created files. Removal requests must identify whether local copies, shared documents and retained recovery points are included.

This public website

This static informational site has no sign-in form, advertising, analytics scripts or application cookies added by the operator. The hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process request information such as IP addresses and browser details under GitHub's privacy statement. Emailing the contact address creates an email record.

Changes and contact

This notice will be updated when relevant practices change. The effective date appears above. For questions or requests, contact t4qy0n@gmail.com.
